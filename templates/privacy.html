Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At VoxAI Studio, we collect personal information you provide directly to us when creating an account, converting text to speech, and configuring settings. This includes your username, email address, password hashes, and conversion inputs/settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to provide the Text-to-Speech synthesis service, store history, track usage statistics, authenticate your sessions, and secure your account. We do not sell or trade your data with third parties.

3. Data Security

We use secure industry-standard hashing algorithms to protect passwords and manage session authorization via JWT tokens. All requests are handled via secure connections. Your uploaded and generated audio files are linked strictly to your user identifier to prevent unauthorized database access.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated conversion history data at any time. This can be done directly from the Account Profile settings page. Once deleted, this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize browser local storage to save your session tokens and interface preference settings (such as dark mode selection). No tracking cookies are used.
